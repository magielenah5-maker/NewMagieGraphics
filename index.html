Can I request both design and website development? +

Absolutely. We can help shape your visual identity and carry it through to a website or digital experience so your brand feels consistent.

Do you develop mobile apps and custom systems? +

Yes. We take on mobile application and custom web-system projects. Share the problem you want to solve, who will use it and the features you have in mind so we can discuss scope and feasibility.

How do I get a quote? +

Tell us what you need, your preferred timeline and any references or examples. We'll discuss the scope and provide a project-specific quotation.

Can you work with a business that is just starting? +

Yes. Whether you're starting with a logo, a simple promotional design or a first website, we can help you choose a sensible starting point.

Where are you based? +

MagieGraphics is based in Rwamukundi, opposite Home Care Clinic, Kabale, Uganda. Get in touch to discuss your project remotely or in person.
